## About

Triips is a flight deal subscription. We watch every route out of our members' home airports, every day, and send an email when a destination drops 50–90 % below its regular price, with every available date. Members book on Skyscanner, our official partner.

Deals are unpredictable and data-driven: we cannot say which destination will drop or when, but one trip pays the membership back many times over — or the member gets a full refund.

Beyond flight deals, we share travel tips from our community to help members spend less once they land: budget-friendly neighbourhoods, hidden gems, smart ways to travel better for less.

**Where we are today**

- **57 departure cities in 17 countries**, across North America, Europe and Oceania. Deals cover **351 destinations in 132 countries**.
- A community of **200,000+ members**, who have saved **$2M+** on flights.

**Our journey**

1. **Triips takes off** — after years of finding cheap flights to see the world, we built an algorithm to do it for everyone. We start in Toronto; 5k members join within the first month.
2. **Expansion across Canada** — Calgary, Montreal and Vancouver; 50k members.
3. **Launch in the United States** — from New York and Miami to San Francisco, with an algorithm tuned to American routes.
4. **100k members** — the Club grows with member recommendations and travel tips.
5. **Major launch in Europe** — our biggest step ever: from Paris and Rome to London.
6. **A fresh new look** — 200k+ members, a brand built around our ambition to become the largest travel community.

## Our Story

Two friends passionate about travel, Omar and Arnaud, explored 100+ countries over the past decade with nothing but a backpack and a shoestring budget. On the same flight, some people pay two or three times more than others for the exact same seat. It all depends on when and how you book. We became obsessed with finding the cheapest flights.

Travel is a form of personal freedom. The biggest barrier is often the price of the ticket. That's exactly why we built Triips.
