## About

Triips is a flight deal subscription service backed by Skyscanner. Using our proprietary detection technology, we continuously monitor millions of flight prices to identify exceptional deals, typically 50-90% off regular prices.

We send alerts 2-3 times per week to our members from their home airport when a destination drops in price. Deals are unpredictable and data-driven. We can't forecast which destinations will drop or when, but we guarantee that you will save money on your next flight ticket.

Beyond flight deals, we share authentic travel tips from our community to help members save once they land: budget-friendly neighborhoods, hidden gems, and smart ways to travel better for less.

**Timeline:**
- **Jan 2025**: Launch in Toronto with first members
- **Mar 2025**: Expansion across Canada (4 cities)
- **May 2025**: Official Skyscanner partnership
- **Jun 2025**: Expansion across the US (13 cities)
- **Now**: Covering 17 cities for 50,000+ members
- **2026**: Worldwide expansion to major cities (coming soon)

## Our Story

Two friends passionate about travel, Omar and Arnaud, explored 100+ countries over the past decade with nothing but a backpack and a shoestring budget. We became obsessed with finding the cheapest flights.

Travel is a form of personal freedom. The biggest barrier is often the price of the ticket. That's exactly why we built Triips!
